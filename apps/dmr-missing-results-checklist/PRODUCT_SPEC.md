# Product spec — DMR Missing Results Checklist

## Target user

Municipal wastewater operators, utility compliance coordinators, and small public-works teams responsible for DMR reporting.

## Problem

DMR preparation often depends on dense permit requirements, lab emails, spreadsheets, and last-minute follow-ups. Missing or pending results create stressful deadline chasing.

## Promise

Paste permit requirements and lab-result rows. Get a draft checklist showing missing, pending, received, and limit-review items.

## Inputs

- permit/reporting requirements text or parameter list
- CSV-style lab/operator result rows
- DMR due date

## Outputs

- readiness status
- missing/pending table
- limit/range review flags
- follow-up checklist
- Markdown report copy
- CSV export

## Non-goals

- no official DMR submission
- no legal/compliance advice
- no permit interpretation authority
- no storage of operational data
